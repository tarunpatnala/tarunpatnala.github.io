CommBank's First Voicebot — Live in < 5 months

  • Helped take Commonwealth Bank's first production Voicebot from design to enterprise launch in under five months.

  • Integrated speech / NLU / dialog with the Genesys contact-centre and CBA backend services for live customer authentication and servicing.

  • Coordinated across CBA, TCS, vendor and infrastructure teams to deliver a fast, audacious launch timeline without compromising quality.

Ceba
Agentic AI

Principal + Connected Agents

Ceba Agentic AI on Copilot Studio

  • Architected and shipped Ceba Agentic AI on Copilot Studio using a Principal + Connected Agents pattern — a central principal agent that delegates user intents to domain-specific connected agents.

  • Defined the inter-agent contract: handoff, context passing, tool invocation, fall-backs and conversation ownership so agents compose cleanly across domains.

  • Wired the agents to enterprise tools, knowledge sources and Azure OpenAI with the guardrails required for a Tier-1 bank.

  • Built the evaluation & observability layer that lets the team measure agent quality and ship safe iterations week-over-week.
