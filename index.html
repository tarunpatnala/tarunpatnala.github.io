// Flagship Quests — last three years

Three years.

Four flagship launches, all shipped by the team into production at a Tier-1 bank with regulator-grade guardrails. No demos. Live customers. Real conversations.

01Migration

We migrated Ceba from Nuance to Microsoft Copilot Studio.

Our team re-platformed CBA's flagship customer-facing chatbot — hundreds of intents, dialog flows and live integrations — from Nuance Mix to Microsoft Copilot Studio. We cut over to production with zero customer-facing downtime, and shaped the testing, evaluation and release-on-rails patterns the team now reuses across CBA's conversational AI portfolio.

Nuance MixCopilot StudioBot FrameworkAzureRegression Evals
Millions of customer conversations a month, re-platformed without downtime
02Launch

We shipped one of Australia's first AI chatbots at enterprise scale.

I'm part of the team that built and deployed one of the first generative-AI chatbots launched at scale in Australian banking. We built conversational flows on top of Azure OpenAI with strict guardrails, grounding and PII handling — and the evaluation harnesses and human-in-the-loop processes that kept it safe to launch and keep improving post go-live, always with the customer experience as the north star.

Azure OpenAIRAGGuardrailsHITL EvalResponsible AI
First Australian enterprise-scale AI chatbot in production banking
03Voicebot

We took CommBank's first Voicebot from idea to live in < 5 months.

I'm part of the team that took Commonwealth Bank's first production Voicebot from blank-page idea to enterprise launch in under five months. We integrated speech, NLU and dialog with the Genesys contact-centre and CBA backends for live customer authentication and servicing — and shipped the voice-UX test strategy: dialog regression, ASR/TTS evals, call-flow drills and load testing prior to cut-over.

VoicebotSpeech ServicesGenesysIVRNLU
< 5 mo end-to-end design → production launch
04Agentic

We built Ceba Agentic AI on Copilot Studio.

Our team built Ceba's Agentic AI on Copilot Studio using a Principal + Connected Agents pattern — a central principal agent that delegates user intents to domain-specific connected agents. We worked together on the inter-agent contract (handoff, context passing, tool invocation, fall-backs, conversation ownership) and the observability layer that lets the team measure agent quality and ship safe iterations week over week.
